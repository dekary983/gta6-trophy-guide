    An ordered path to the platinum, built from the official trophy descriptions and the published roadmap sources cited under each stage. Roughly 200 hours, one playthrough, no online play — and exactly one trophy that can be lost, flagged in stage 2.

    One trophy is flagged as potentially missable by every published guide we cite. Five more describe one-off moments, so this app flags them “watch” — take the moment when it comes, and keep manual saves at chapter boundaries so a miss is recoverable.
